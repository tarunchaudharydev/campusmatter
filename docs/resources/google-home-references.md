## Research Focus

The CampusMatter project studies existing Matter-enabled ecosystems such as Google Home to understand interoperability standards, local communication architecture, and smart automation infrastructure.

The analysis helps identify opportunities for scalable campus-oriented smart infrastructure optimization.
