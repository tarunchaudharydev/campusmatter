## CampusMatter Research Focus

CampusMatter explores the Matter ecosystem to build a scalable smart hostel automation and energy optimization infrastructure capable of supporting:

- Centralized monitoring
- Distributed ESP32 smart nodes
- Occupancy-based automation
- Secure local communication
- Real-time energy analytics
- Smart appliance automation

The project focuses on combining Matter interoperability with smart campus infrastructure management to create an efficient, scalable, and research-oriented IoT ecosystem.
